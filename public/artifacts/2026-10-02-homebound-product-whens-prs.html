Where the review went deep

Scope and product semantics

  • Pressed on whether a material used in two ItemSlots was being counted across slots; the result needed per-slot quantity math and cross-slot condition merging.
  • Asked for a worked negative-quantity example distinguishing an invalid slot total from one negative material in an otherwise positive slot.
  • Examined whether availableWhen and productsWhen were derived in one pass, then saved as separate outputs.

Tests and maintainability

  • Requested leaner test graphs: inline the two-use fixture, share TLI setup, and test both package options.
  • Challenged unnecessary location/UOM construction and factory overrides while retaining the fields needed to keep two variants in one ItemSlot.
  • Refined names, examples, and error diagnostics so future readers can distinguish a candidate option, an effective selection, and the slot being evaluated.

Evidence beyond comment counts

Performance and result comparison

The foundation PR description reports a local production-copy comparison across five offerings and 1,907 package options. It records a roughly 8–24× improvement versus the warm previous resolver, while disclosing seven differing product results and order changes for roughly 4% of options.

Historical migration validation

The backfill PR description reports running the job over 94 Product Offerings and checking the version rows read by 1,175 target aggregate versions. Its approximately 55-minute job runtime is execution time, not estimated engineering time.
